This file makes this copy of OpenRB-338 portable.

While portable.txt lies next to OpenRB-338.exe, the program keeps its settings
(data\ui.toml) and its logs (data\logs) in the folder data beside it, and writes nothing to
AppData. The whole folder can live on a USB stick or anywhere you can write to (not in
C:\Program Files, which needs administrator rights to write).

The songs and mods folders you choose inside this folder are remembered relative to it, so
they still work when the stick has another drive letter. Folders elsewhere are remembered as
full paths.

Delete this file to keep the settings in %APPDATA%\OpenRB-338 and the logs in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenRB-338\logs instead, as an installed program does.
