## Description

OpenRB-338 usable with a screen reader, NVDA on Windows first: every control named and operable from the keyboard, the patterns readable and programmable, files, mods, messages and windows spoken, quiet while the music plays unless asked. A "Screen reader mode" that leaves the app exactly as upstream when off; the engine is not touched. The fork's milestone (the user, @dengopaiv, 2026-11-05). The analysis is docs/accessibility.md, the plan with every task, decision and the NVDA checklist docs/accessibility_plan.md.
